    How to survive

    • Complete every task on a floor, then call the elevator and survive until it arrives.
    • Stay quiet. Sprinting is loud, crouching is nearly silent. Your flashlight makes you easier to spot.
    • Hide in lockers — but if they see you climb in, they'll drag you out.
    • Weapons are rare. Search drawers, cabinets and crates. They only stun the monsters.
    • Downed teammates can be revived — hold E on them.
